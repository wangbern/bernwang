:::row
title: Intro
image: bubu.jpg, maggie.png, swaggy.png, yoki.png, benoit.png, icee.png, nixie.png, pixie.png, bubus.jpg
side: full
bullet: Each Labubu gets a life story, and an outfit from scraps. #narrative

I create a story and personality for each Labubu, imagining what they would wear or use in their daily life. It’s a great creative hobby for whimsy. I also often recycle everyday items, like a pen cap, bra insert, or the lone sock. #narrative

People have told me sell the costumes but I could not predict where I would get the best materials next. Plus, each take an embarrassingly amount of time and brainspace.
:::

title: Maggie
images: maggie.png, maggie1.jpg, maggie2.jpg, maggie3.jpg
side: right
bullet: A monocled enchantress. Sword from a barrette and washers, cape and suit hand sewn.

A magical monocled swashbuckling enchantress. The sword is made from a barret clip and washers; the cape and brocade suit hand sewn. If siblings order is real, Maggie is an older sister.

I would love her luxurious cape for myself, and peep the pom pom leaf boots.

:::row
title: Swaggy
images: swaggy.png, swaggy1.jpg, swaggy2.jpg, swaggy3.jpg
side: left
bullet: First upcycled outfit. Travel-Tabasco wine, yogurt-lid shades, the cool girl on the block.

My first outfit I ever made by upcycling premade doll clothes. Swaggy is meant to be THE cool girl on the block: the wine bottle is made from a travel tabasco bottle and their shades from the back of a yogurt lid.

:::row
title: Yoki
images: yoki.png, yoki4.jpg, yoki2.jpg, yoki3.jpg
side: right
bullet: Commissioned to match her owner after KPop Demon Hunters: studs, beanie, tote, pierced ears.

This character was commissioned by a friend who both wanted to match with her Labubu and had just watched Kpop Demon Hunters. Yoki has pierced ears, studs on leather, and chic beanie with casual tote bag combo - the ultimate modern Saja Boy. Actually, I tried making the gat, the Korean traditional hat, for Yoki but a beanie ended up looking better.
:::

:::row
title: Benoit Blabubu
images: benoit.png, benoit1.jpg, benoit2.jpg, benoit3.jpg
side: left
bullet: A noir commission that became a Knives Out detective, trench coat with working pockets.

Commissioned by another friend who is a big fan of the noir genre. It ended up closer to the Knives Out detective but features the most elaborate tailoring yet with a fully functional trench coat that ties and has hidden pockets. The hat is stitched from real leather scraps. Spot the details; there's even a rolled up newspaper in his coat.

:::row
title: Iccee
images: icee.png, icee2.jpg, icee3.jpg
side: right
bullet: My self-insert, in a Japanese handkerchief dress and a crown of broken earrings.

Of the labubus, this one is my self insert. Iccee is iced out and wearing a dress made from a handkerchief I got during a Japanese exchange program. I loved the print, but never used it as a handkerchief. The crown is made from a single earring, a hoop earring, and other broken dangly jewelry bits.

Canonically, Iccee carries a woven tote full of picnic foods and fruit.
:::

bullet: A space loving blueberry fairy with beads and tiny wings.

Pixie features fully beaded ears, a crocheted crossbody, and scavenged acorn buckle. With such tiny wings, she can’t exactly fly but that doesn’t stop her longing for the stars and space. Her bright blue fit is made up of bra inserts.

Pixie was originally light green but I wanted to try dyeing the plush... somehow, she ended up gray. The hat is what I'd like to wear if I was a Brimmed Hat in Witch Hat Atelier.
